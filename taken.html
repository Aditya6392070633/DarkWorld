OBJECTIVE
Eliminate ALL hostiles in each wave. Clear all 3 waves per level to unlock the next safehouse.

Start with 100 HP. Each bullet = -10 HP. Reach zero and the mission fails permanently.
🔫
AMMO
Pistol holds 12 rounds. Press R to reload. Auto-reloads on empty. Grab A drops for +6 rounds.
💊
DROPS
Enemies drop ammo packs (30%) and medkits +25HP (10%) on death. Walk over to collect.

Each kill = 100 × Level points. Level 5 enemies = 500 pts each. Maximize your total!
🧠
ENEMY AI
Enemies patrol until within 8 tiles — then they chase and shoot. Later levels = faster & tougher.

MOVEMENT
